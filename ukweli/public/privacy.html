Privacy Policy

ImaarishaSRHR Collective — last updated 1 July 2026

This policy explains how the ImaarishaSRHR platform handles personal data. It covers the Collective Hub (the members’ operations room) and UkweliSRHR (the youth-facing information PWA).

UkweliSRHR is designed to be used anonymously — no account, no name required — so young people can ask sexual-and-reproductive-health questions safely. Please don’t include your real name or contact details in a question.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Community Media Trust, P.O. Box 9190–00300, Kirichwa Road, ADK House 1, Kilimani, Nairobi, Kenya. Privacy questions and requests: hello@imaarishasrhr.org. We process data under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and, where applicable, the GDPR.

2. What we collect and why

DataWhenWhy (lawful basis)
Member account: name, email, organisationWhen you sign up to the Collective HubYour consent, to give you a member account and list your organisation in the Directory (after admin approval)
Forum posts, resources, marketplace listings, field-activity photos/videos (UnaDO?)When you post themYour consent; content you choose to share with the network
Weekly digest email addressWhen you subscribeYour consent
UkweliSRHR questionsWhen submittedAnonymous by design — we ask you not to include identifying information

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Where your data lives and who can see it

Data is stored in Supabase and served via Vercel. Row-level security controls access: member-only content is visible only to signed-in members; field-activity uploads appear only after an admin approves them; anonymous youth questions are seen only by the answering team. Organisations appear in the public Directory only after admin approval.

4. On-device data

Your language preference and similar settings are stored only in your browser and never leave your device.

5. How long we keep it

Account and content data is kept while your membership is active and deleted on request. Removing your profile from the members list is available in the app; full account/login deletion can be requested from us.

6. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data — email hello@imaarishasrhr.org. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), Kenya (odpc.go.ke).

7. Security

We use HTTPS, database row-level security, admin-gated moderation, and modern security headers. Youth users can leave the site instantly with the quick-exit control.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be noted here with a new date.
